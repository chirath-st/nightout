How access will work

Getting an account

  1. Email us from your bar (see Contact). We check that you run it.
  2. We create a manager account for your bar and send you a setup link.
  3. You set a password and turn on two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, with us on a short call.

What keeps it safe

  • Each account sees only its own bar, checked on our server every time, not just in the page.
  • A password plus a code from an authenticator app.
  • Signed out when the tab closes, unless you choose “Keep me signed in on this computer”.
  • Totals only: no names, accounts or locations of guests.
