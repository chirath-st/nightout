its bar, except where noted. No bar has given permission or is affiliated with Night Out, and permission will be
requested before any launch. Downloaded 2026-10-17 with the user's approval. Logos were trimmed and centered on
256 px squares. Photos were cropped to 3:2 and resized (at most 1200 px wide, JPEG quality 78).
