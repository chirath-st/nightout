# Night Out — Sample Preview (public-demo-20261027-2)

The same app as the iPhone build, from the same source files. Everything in src/ and styles/app.css, styles/tokens.css is
byte-identical to the iPhone app's bundle. Differences are host-only: this page's index.html (security policy, banner,
version), the banner style, this README, the manifest and robots.txt, and no bar photos or logos (illustrations and
initials instead).

Waits, deals, events, passes, points, rewards, the Plus plan, people and the Business view are sample data. No bar is
affiliated with or has endorsed Night Out; the Business view is a demo login that grants nothing. Nothing is sent or
charged: there is no server, and the page's security policy blocks uploads. A photo taken in Scan stays in this tab's
memory until its report is added or closed; photos added in Business view are let go when you leave it. Neither is
saved, and no photo is analyzed or counted by software. Saved bars, points and settings stay in this browser. The 21+
screen is an age statement, not identity verification.
